# Message Spine

## Core truth
This is for dog owners who want their feeding choices to reflect their values without feeling reckless about their dog's health.

## Target audience
- values-driven dog owners
- vegan or vegetarian households
- sustainability-minded pet owners

## Sharp pain statement
The hardest part is not finding dog food.
The hardest part is trusting that a plant-based option can align with your ethics and still feel nutritionally responsible.

## Desired shift or outcome
Move from:
- ethical tension
- feeding anxiety
- repeat doubt

to:
- ethical alignment
- nutritional reassurance
- subscription confidence

## Strongest language to preserve
- values without compromise
- feel good about what you feed your dog
- nutritional peace of mind
- food that matches your ethics

## Homepage message implications
The homepage should:
- lead with trust and reassurance
- make the ethical alignment feel calm, not activist
- treat health credibility as central, not secondary
- avoid overclaiming health benefits

## Key trust question to answer
How can I believe this is a responsible thing to feed my dog?

## Weak directions to avoid
- preachy moral language
- vague sustainability copy
- overconfident health claims
- subscription-first framing before trust is established
