# Proof Map

## What the homepage can honestly carry now
- the emotional and ethical tension is real
- trust is the main barrier
- convenience matters after trust

## What the homepage does not yet have
- veterinary proof
- nutritional certifications
- customer testimonials
- comparative product data

## Safe credibility moves
- speak carefully about nutritional completeness only if framed as a requirement, not a claim
- acknowledge the seriousness of the feeding decision
- keep trust language calm and precise

## Risky moves to avoid
- medical or health superiority claims
- dramatic moral language
- any claim that sounds like evidence without evidence

## What the hero can say safely
- values and feeding responsibility both matter

## What the trust section can say safely
- dog owners deserve both ethical alignment and nutritional confidence
- this product is built around taking that concern seriously

## Current judgment
Thin proof layer, but the guardrails are clear.
