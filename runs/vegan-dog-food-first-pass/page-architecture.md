# Homepage Architecture

## Homepage job
Help a values-driven dog owner feel that this service could be both ethically aligned and nutritionally responsible.

## Visitor state on arrival
Curious, skeptical and protective.
They may like the ethical idea already, but they need reassurance before they can trust the product.

## Core message to land first
You should not have to choose between your values and feeling responsible about what you feed your dog.

## Supporting messages
- plant-based can still be serious and nutritionally complete
- this is built for dog owners who care deeply
- subscription is convenience after trust, not before

## Trust question the page must answer
Why should I trust this as real dog food, not just a nice-sounding idea?

## Sections the homepage likely needs
1. hero: values + reassurance
2. problem/tension section
3. product explanation section
4. trust / nutrition section
5. subscription convenience section
6. CTA section

## Proof burden per section
- hero: calm promise, no inflated health claim
- problem: emotional truth of ethical tension
- product: explain what the food is in simple terms
- trust: strongest available credibility language
- convenience: light subscription value, after trust is earned

## Current judgment
Clear and usable for a homepage draft.
