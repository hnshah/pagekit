# Wedge Definition Brief

## Working decision
Treat this product as a service for dog owners who want their feeding choices to reflect their values without feeling reckless about their dog's health.

## First audience hypothesis
Primary audience:
- values-driven dog owners
- vegan or vegetarian households
- sustainability-minded pet owners

## Sharp pain framing
The pain is not "I need dog food shipped to my house."
The pain is: "I want a food choice that aligns with my ethics, but I do not want to feel like I am gambling with my dog's health."

## Homepage implication
The homepage should optimize for:
- trust
- nutritional reassurance
- ethical alignment
- calm credibility

## Claims we still cannot make
- broad health superiority claims
- guaranteed outcomes
- medical claims without evidence
