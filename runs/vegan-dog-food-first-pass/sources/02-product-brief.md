# Product Brief

## Product
A vegan dog food subscription service.

## Working product description
A direct-to-consumer subscription service that delivers plant-based dog food on a recurring basis.

## Likely category tension
This is not a normal convenience-only subscription product.
The core tension is trust.
People are not just choosing food delivery.
They are deciding whether a plant-based option is safe, healthy and responsible for their dog.

## Likely buyer
A dog owner who is already values-driven around food, sustainability or animal welfare, but still wants to feel responsible and evidence-based about what they feed their dog.

## Likely homepage challenge
The homepage cannot rely on generic subscription language.
It needs to handle:
- trust
- health credibility
- emotional concern
- ethical motivation

## Constraints
- do not invent veterinary proof
- do not make unsupported health claims
- do not assume every dog owner is already open to vegan dog food
