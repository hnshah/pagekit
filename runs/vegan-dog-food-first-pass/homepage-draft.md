# Homepage Draft

## Hero headline
Dog food that matches your values and respects your dog's needs.

## Hero subhead
A vegan dog food subscription for people who want to feed their dog in a way that feels ethically aligned, nutritionally responsible and easy to keep up with.

## Primary CTA
See what's inside

## Problem section
For many dog owners, the hardest part is not finding food.
It is trusting that the food reflects what they care about without feeling like a gamble.

If you care about animal welfare, sustainability or what goes into every bowl, the decision gets heavier, not lighter.

## Product section
This service is built for dog owners who want a plant-based option that feels serious.
Not trendy. Not careless. Not built on guilt.

The goal is simple:
make it easier to choose food that fits your values, feel confident about that choice and keep it consistent through a subscription that removes repeat friction.

## Trust / credibility section
Feeding your dog is too important for vague promises.
That is why the page needs to earn trust through clarity.

The product is being framed around a real concern:
dog owners want ethical alignment, but they also want to feel responsible, informed and careful.

## Convenience section
Once trust is there, subscription becomes the easy part.
You stay stocked without having to revisit the same decision every time.

## Closing CTA section
If you want your dog's food to reflect your values without feeling careless about their health, this is the kind of option worth looking at more closely.

CTA: See what's inside

## Current judgment
This is a usable first draft.
It is calmer and more trust-aware than generic subscription copy.
It still needs stronger product-detail and trust-detail layers before it feels truly strong.
