# Source Capture

## What we have
- Committed product brief for Verdel (fictional training object with concrete facts)
- Pattern-level signal from public vegan dog food community discussion: Amazon reviews, Reddit (r/vegan, r/dogs, r/veganpets), vet forum Q&As, indie owner blogs
- Two prior PageKit runs on vegan dog food available for reference (first-pass and fresh-run)
- Publicly observable competitor landscape: V-dog, Wild Earth, Halo Holistic, Benevo, Ami

## What we do not have
- Primary owner interviews
- Real sales call transcripts or support tickets
- Verified palatability data or feeding trial results beyond what the product brief specifies
- Actual owner testimonials

## Signal quality
Pattern-level only. The signal doc draws on repeated patterns across public sources, not verified individual accounts. Downstream claims stay within what is repeatedly observable, not what has been confirmed person by person.

## What the product brief settles
The product brief (02-product-brief.md) commits to concrete facts that the run can treat as ground truth. Any claim that exceeds those facts gets a verification flag.
