# Product Brief — Verdel

## Name
Verdel

## What it is
Dry kibble dog food. Complete and balanced for adult dogs. No animal-derived ingredients.

## Formulation
- Grain-inclusive (oats, brown rice) — not grain-free
- Primary protein sources: peas, lentils, chickpeas, nutritional yeast
- Added amino acids: taurine and L-carnitine
- Added DHA from algae oil
- Reviewed by Dr. Sarah Kim, DACVN (board-certified veterinary nutritionist) before launch

## Nutritional standard
Meets AAFCO nutritional profiles for adult maintenance. This is stated on the bag and on the product page.

## Palatability guarantee
30-day money-back guarantee. No return of the product required. Full refund on first bag if the dog will not eat it.

## Transition support
Printed transition guide in every box. Recommends a 10-day gradual mix-in.

## Packaging and price
- 22 lb bag: $59
- Approximate 30-day supply for a 40 lb adult dog
- Available in 5 lb (trial size, $18) and 44 lb (subscription, $109/month)

## Shipping
- Free shipping on orders $40 and above
- Ships within 2 business days from US warehouse
- Delivered in 3–5 business days

## Availability
Online only. Sold at verdeldogfood.com. No retail distribution in this pass.

## What is not claimed
- No "vet approved" on the bag (formulation was reviewed by a DACVN, which is noted on the packaging as "formulated with veterinary nutritionist review")
- No feeding trial data beyond AAFCO nutrient profiles
- No published palatability rate or percentage
- No sustainability certifications in place yet
