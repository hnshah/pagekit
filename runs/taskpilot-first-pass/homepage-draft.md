# TaskPilot Homepage Draft

## Hero headline
See where important work is starting to slip.

## Hero subhead
TaskPilot helps founders, operators and small-team leads spot momentum loss earlier, reduce status-chasing and keep execution clearer once work is already in motion.

## Primary CTA
See how TaskPilot works

## Problem section
The issue is rarely getting work onto a list.
The issue is what happens next.

Once projects are moving, it gets harder to see what is drifting, what is blocked and what needs attention now.
Teams end up spending too much time chasing updates, checking status and reacting late.

## Product section
TaskPilot is designed to make execution easier to read.

It helps you spot slippage earlier, keep follow-through visible and reduce coordination drag around the work that matters most.

If your team already has tools for planning, assigning and tracking work, TaskPilot is meant to help with the part that often stays blurry once execution begins: where momentum is weakening, where attention is needed and what should be moved forward now.

## Trust / credibility section
TaskPilot is built around a simple idea:
planning tools tell you what should happen.
TaskPilot is focused on helping you see what is actually starting to slip.

That is the difference this page is trying to make clear.

## Closing CTA section
If your team already has plans and tools but still loses momentum on important work, TaskPilot is meant to help you see that earlier and respond with more clarity.
