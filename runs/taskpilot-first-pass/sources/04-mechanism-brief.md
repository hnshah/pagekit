# TaskPilot Mechanism Brief

## Why this source exists
The first homepage draft exposed the main missing layer:
we have pain, audience and wedge, but the product mechanism is still too vague.

## Working mechanism hypothesis
TaskPilot should be treated as a product that helps people see when important work is losing momentum.

That means its job is likely to help users:
- see what matters now
- notice what is slipping
- reduce status-chasing
- keep follow-through visible

## What the product likely does in user terms
Without overclaiming, the homepage should be able to imply that TaskPilot helps a user:
- identify which work needs attention now
- see where work is stuck or drifting
- understand where follow-through is weakening
- spend less time asking for updates manually

## What the mechanism is not
Do not frame it as:
- a generic to-do list
- a broad project management suite
- AI magic for all work
- full automation of execution

## Homepage implication
The product section should speak in user-visible outcomes:
- what becomes easier to notice
- what becomes easier to act on
- what becomes less noisy

## Constraint
Until we have stronger product detail, the mechanism should stay simple and legible.
It should not sound like a hidden system doing mysterious things behind the scenes.
