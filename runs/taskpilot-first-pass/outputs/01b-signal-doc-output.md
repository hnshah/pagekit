# TaskPilot Signal Doc — Second Pass

## 1. What the product appears to be now
TaskPilot appears to be a work-momentum product for people who already have tasks, plans and tools, but still lose important work between planning and execution.

That makes it feel less like generic task management software and closer to a product built around follow-through, visibility and coordination clarity.

## 2. Clearest first audience
Strongest current first audience:
- founders
- operators
- small-team leads

Why this audience feels strongest:
- they sit closest to priority drift
- they feel coordination overhead directly
- they are often the ones chasing status across too many moving parts

## 3. Sharpest pain framing
The problem is not that work never gets planned.
The problem is that important work loses momentum.

It gets delayed by handoff gaps, hidden blockers, unclear priorities and constant status-checking.
By the time the problem becomes visible, the cost is already real.

## 4. Desired outcomes
- know what matters now
- keep important work moving
- catch slippage earlier
- reduce status-chasing
- create more confidence in follow-through
