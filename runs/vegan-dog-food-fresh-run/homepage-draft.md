## Draft notes

**What this draft does differently from a values-first approach:**
- Nutrition lands in section 1; values land in section 4
- The palatability objection is named and answered before the emotional case is made
- The values section earns its place rather than opening with it
- The hero line ("in that order") signals brand priority without dismissing the values case

**What this draft needs before publication:**
- AAFCO status confirmed and inserted in hero sub-headline and section 2
- Protein sources, amino acid additions, and veterinary nutritionist involvement confirmed and inserted in section 2
- Palatability version selected (A, B, or C) based on what the brand actually offers
- Real owner stories inserted in section 5 — not generic testimonials
- Product name inserted throughout

**Key test for the hero line:**
Read the headline aloud to a values-led owner who is also worried about their dog's nutrition. Does it produce relief ("finally, a brand that takes the health question seriously") or skepticism ("another brand saying what they think I want to hear")? If the latter, the sub-headline is not doing enough work — the specifics need to be tighter.

**Upstream recommendation:**
Before publishing, run 3-5 owner interviews split between values-led and health-led visitors. Test whether the nutrition-first order performs better than values-first for conversion. The hypothesis in this run is that it does — but it should be tested, not assumed.
