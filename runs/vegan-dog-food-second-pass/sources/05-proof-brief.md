# Proof Brief — Second Pass

## What Kind Bowl has as proof today

### Formulation-level proof
- recipe formulated to AAFCO adult-maintenance nutrient profile
- formulation reviewed and iterated with a board-certified veterinary nutritionist (ACVN diplomate)
- synthetic amino acid completion (methionine, lysine) documented in formulation
- taurine and L-carnitine documented in formulation
- algae-based DHA source documented
- vegan-compatible vitamin D3 source documented

### Testing-level proof
- third-party lab test of amino acid profile against AAFCO minimums
- per-batch nutrient-level testing before shipment

### People-level proof
- the name and credentials of the consulting veterinary nutritionist are disclosable

## What Kind Bowl does not have

### Feeding trial
- no AAFCO feeding trial (a feeding trial is a separate 6-month animal study and is more expensive than formulation-based compliance)

### Long-term outcome data
- no peer-reviewed study of long-term health outcomes in dogs fed Kind Bowl

### Broad veterinary endorsement
- no general veterinary endorsement beyond the single consulting nutritionist
- no breed-specific guidance
- no age-specific guidance beyond "adult"

### Customer proof
- no meaningful body of customer reviews yet
- no documented case studies
- no longitudinal owner-reported outcomes

## What this means for the first-page

### Safe proof moves
- name the specific AAFCO standard met (adult maintenance)
- name the credential of the consulting nutritionist (ACVN diplomate)
- name the third-party amino acid testing
- name the per-batch testing
- show the formulation's key supplements and what they do nutritionally

### Unsafe proof moves
- "vet approved" as a headline
- any "most dogs thrive" phrasing
- any "studies show" language that references studies Kind Bowl did not do
- any implication that Kind Bowl is for puppies, seniors or therapeutic use

### Honest acknowledgements the page can make
- this is adult-maintenance food, not an all-life-stages product
- this is not a replacement for veterinary advice on medically restricted diets
- this is a nutritional-adequacy claim, not a health-outcome claim
