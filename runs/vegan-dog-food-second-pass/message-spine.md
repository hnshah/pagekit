# Message Spine — Second Pass

## Core truth
Kind Bowl is a plant-based adult-maintenance dog food formulated to AAFCO nutrient profiles with a board-certified veterinary nutritionist and verified by third-party amino acid testing. Not a lifestyle product, not therapeutic, not for puppies or medically restricted dogs at launch.

## Target audience
Adult-dog owners who are vegan or vegetarian themselves and have looked into plant-based dog food at least once — specifically the subset who bounced off the soft-evidence problem in the category.

## Sharp pain statement
The hard part is not finding plant-based dog food.
The hard part is telling which plant-based dog food has taken nutritional adequacy seriously, because category marketing hides which brands have done the work.

## Desired shift
From: "plant-based dog food is risky and the category is noisy"
To: "this specific brand has taken the nutritional adequacy question seriously in a way I can check, and I know what it is and is not for."

## Strongest language to preserve
- formulated by a board-certified veterinary nutritionist
- AAFCO adult-maintenance nutrient profile
- third-party amino acid testing
- for adult dogs
- what we have done and what we have not done
- nutritional adequacy (not superiority)

## Page-level message implications
- lead with nutritional credibility, not ethics
- name the specific AAFCO flavor of compliance (formulation + testing, not feeding trial)
- name what has not been done
- make the adult-only scope visible
- name the consulting nutritionist credential, not "vets" generically
- no bare "vet approved"
- no individual-dog outcome claims
- do not frame Kind Bowl as superior to conventional food on health

## Key trust question
How do I know this is actually nutritionally defensible for my adult dog, in a way I can verify rather than take on faith?

## Weak directions to avoid
- leading with ethics or sustainability
- "guilt-free" or "feel good" framing
- bare "vet approved"
- implied health superiority
- subscription-first framing before trust is earned
- any phrasing that lets the adult-only scope slip

## Judgment
Strong enough to advance to the first-page decision. Intentionally silent on which page should come first.
