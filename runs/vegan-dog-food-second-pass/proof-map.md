# Proof Map — Second Pass

## Page
Kind Bowl nutritional-adequacy page (not a homepage).

## What the page can honestly claim now
- formulated to meet the AAFCO Dog Food Nutrient Profile for adult maintenance
- formulated with a board-certified veterinary nutritionist (ACVN diplomate)
- amino acid profile verified against AAFCO minimums by a third-party lab
- every batch is tested for nutrient levels before shipment
- for adult dogs
- compliance is by formulation and third-party testing, not by AAFCO feeding trial
- specific supplementation grounded in the mechanism brief (methionine, lysine, taurine, L-carnitine, algae DHA, vegan D3)

## What the page cannot support
- long-term health outcomes
- comparisons to conventional food on health outcomes
- suitability for puppies, seniors, pregnant, nursing or therapeutically restricted dogs
- broad veterinary endorsement
- customer satisfaction or palatability (no meaningful corpus)
- any "dogs thrive" phrasing

## Safe credibility moves
- name the AAFCO standard in full
- name the compliance flavor explicitly
- name the nutritionist's credential (ACVN diplomate) and the role (consulting, ongoing)
- name the third-party testing practice
- name the per-batch testing practice
- explicitly section off the "what we have not done" list
- explicitly section off the "not for" list

## Risky or inflated moves to avoid
- bare "vet approved"
- "clinically tested" / "clinically proven"
- "healthiest"
- health-outcome comparisons to conventional kibble
- equating formulation compliance to a feeding trial
- "all life stages"
- "dogs thrive" or "dogs love it" without data
- individual-dog anecdotes as proof
- "backed by science" for work Kind Bowl did not conduct

## Where proof lands, section by section

### Scope statement
- positioning only; safe line: "plant-based dog food for adult dogs, formulated to meet AAFCO adult-maintenance nutrient requirements."
- must not: introduce lifestyle or values framing here.

### AAFCO compliance, made specific
- exact profile named
- exact compliance flavor named (formulation + third-party testing, not feeding trial)
- must not: say "AAFCO approved" or "AAFCO certified."

### Who formulated it
- credential named in full (ACVN diplomate)
- role named (consulting on formulation, ongoing)
- must not: generalize to "our vets" or "team of veterinary experts."

### Mechanism
- specific supplementation (methionine, lysine, taurine, L-carnitine)
- algae DHA, vegan-compatible D3
- must not: claim superiority or uniqueness.

### Testing
- third-party amino acid test against AAFCO minimums
- per-batch nutrient testing
- must not: say "rigorously tested" without naming what and how often.

### What we have not done
- no AAFCO feeding trial
- no long-term outcome studies
- not therapeutic
- not for puppies, pregnant, nursing or therapeutically restricted dogs
- must not: soften with "yet" unless there is a real plan.

### Unhyped next step
- link to a deeper resource that continues the credibility story
- must not: be a subscription offer or discount.

## Judgment
The proof map is narrow on purpose. If the draft tries to widen the page beyond this, it will overclaim.
