# First-Page Decision — Second Pass

## Candidate first page
A **nutritional-adequacy page**.
Working title: "How we built a plant-based dog food to an AAFCO adult-maintenance standard."

This is not a homepage.

## Why this page first
- the spine's one real trust question is nutritional-credibility, not brand awareness
- a homepage rhythm (hero, category benefits, subscription CTA) invites the category's default skepticism, it does not answer it
- a page that leads with the mechanism and the scope can answer the actual question on the first visit
- it also differentiates most sharply from category competitors, whose marketing is softest on exactly this

## Page job
Show, in one read:
- the specific AAFCO flavor of compliance (formulation + third-party amino acid testing, not feeding trial)
- the mechanism that makes plant-based formulation meet adult-maintenance requirements
- the consulting veterinary nutritionist credential, specifically
- the scope (adult dogs only)
- what Kind Bowl has not done
- and leave the reader able to decide whether to continue

The page is not trying to sell a subscription on this visit.

## Visitor state on arrival
- already vegan or vegetarian themselves
- has looked at plant-based dog food before
- probably bounced off at least one plant-based brand whose claims felt soft
- primed to detect marketing language
- wants specifics, not promises

## Main trust burden
Convince a skeptical values-aligned adult-dog owner that this plant-based food has taken nutritional adequacy seriously in a way they can verify, without using the category's soft trust language.

## Main proof burden
- specific AAFCO profile met
- specific compliance path used
- specific credential of the consulting nutritionist
- specific supplementation strategy
- specific per-batch testing practice
- specific scope limits
- explicit naming of what has not been done

## What this rules out for now
- a homepage-first approach
- opening on ethics, sustainability or lifestyle framing
- opening on subscription mechanics
- requiring breadth across puppies, seniors or therapeutic diets
- requiring broad veterinary endorsement beyond the single consulting nutritionist

## Candidates considered and rejected
- **Homepage**: rejected. Generic rhythm does not answer the trust question and invites default skepticism.
- **"Why plant-based dog food" category-explainer**: rejected. Argues for the category, not for Kind Bowl; drifts into advocacy.
- **Ingredient / formula detail page**: weaker. Shows what but not how or credential. Ingredient detail belongs inside the chosen page, not as the first page.
- **Founder / brand story**: rejected. Story cannot carry nutritional credibility for this audience.

## Risk this decision introduces
The chosen page can slide into technical-explainer mode and lose a reader who wanted a simpler entry. The argument shape step has to protect against this by front-loading scope and credential, not burying them.

## Judgment
Strong decision. The page name is narrower than "homepage" and the job is narrower than a homepage's job — which is what this object needs.
