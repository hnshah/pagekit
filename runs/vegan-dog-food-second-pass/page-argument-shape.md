# Page Argument Shape — Second Pass

## Page
Kind Bowl nutritional-adequacy page ("How we built a plant-based dog food to an AAFCO adult-maintenance standard").

## What the visitor needs to understand first
In the first screen, three things:
- this is plant-based dog food for adult dogs only
- formulated to the AAFCO adult-maintenance nutrient profile with a board-certified veterinary nutritionist
- this page exists to show the work, not to sell

## What needs to be earned next
In order:
- which AAFCO profile and which compliance flavor (formulation + third-party testing, not feeding trial)
- the consulting nutritionist's credential (ACVN diplomate), named specifically
- how plant-based formulation hits adult-maintenance requirements (the mechanism)
- what is tested and how often
- what Kind Bowl has not done

## Argument order
1. Scope and positioning, one honest sentence
2. What the product was formulated to
3. Who formulated it
4. The mechanism
5. Testing
6. What has not been done and what is not being claimed
7. A small, unhyped next step

## Section plan
- Scope statement
- AAFCO compliance, made specific
- Who formulated it
- The mechanism
- Testing
- What we have not done
- A small, unhyped next step

## Sections that would be unnecessary or harmful
- hero with brand promise
- founder story
- lifestyle imagery section
- ethics or sustainability section
- customer testimonials (no meaningful review corpus yet)
- subscription-value section
- pricing section
- category-default "benefits" section

## Where trust should land
On the credentialing and compliance sections. Not on brand voice or aesthetics.

## Where proof should land
- AAFCO profile compliance, named specifically
- ACVN diplomate credential, named specifically
- third-party amino acid testing, named specifically
- per-batch testing, named specifically
- the "what we have not done" section, which is itself a proof-honesty move

## Drafting constraints
- no bare "vet approved"
- no "clinically proven"
- no health-superiority comparisons
- no life-stage breadth language
- no outcome claims for individual dogs
- do not lead with ethics
- no "guilt-free" / "feel good" framing
- do not bury the adult-only scope
- do not hide the not-yet-done list
- keep headings boring — content carries the weight
- the page can be longer than a homepage; it cannot be wider

## Judgment
Strong shape. Visibly not a homepage rhythm. Main risk is reading too technical; mitigation is front-loading scope and credential.
