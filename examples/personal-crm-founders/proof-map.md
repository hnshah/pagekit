# Proof Map — Me CRM

## What the page can honestly claim

From the product brief (confirmed):
- Relationship state card tracks: last contact date, conversation notes, open follow-ups, next step
- Weekly digest: email every Monday surfacing relationships with no contact in 30+ days
- Snooze/reminder: "check back in X days" per relationship
- iOS app for immediate post-conversation note capture
- Gmail import for initial contact history
- No pipeline, no deal stages (intentionally absent)
- Free tier: up to 25 relationships
- Solo: $19/month, unlimited relationships, reminders, weekly digest
- Team: $49/month, up to 5 people, shared relationship context
- 14-day trial, no credit card required
- Contact data not sold or shared
- Data export (CSV) always available, any plan
- SOC 2 Type II in progress (not certified)
- Hosted on AWS, encrypted at rest and in transit
- 200 founders on waitlist (pre-launch signal)
- Built by a former seed-stage founder

Pattern-level (not Me CRM user data):
- The failure modes named (going dark, context scatter, follow-up debt) are accurate as category-level observations about the founder experience — not claims about Me CRM users.

## What the page does not yet have proof for

- No testimonials or user quotes (pre-launch)
- No usage data or outcome data
- SOC 2 certification (in progress only)
- Product value claims beyond the product's features and structure

## Safe credibility moves

- Name the failure modes precisely — pattern accuracy is a credibility signal
- State features specifically (the state card fields, digest cadence, iOS app) — specificity beats capability language
- Cite 200 waitlist as early interest signal, not a value proof
- State trial terms plainly — the terms themselves signal confidence
- State data posture plainly — plain statement, not a trust performance

## Risky moves to avoid

- Any sentence implying Me CRM users have better outcomes
- "Most founders find..." or "Founders typically..." applied to Me CRM users
- Implying SOC 2 is completed
- Outcome claims ("follow up more consistently," "keep relationships warmer")
